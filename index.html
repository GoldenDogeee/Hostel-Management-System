Hostel Management System

A calmer way to run a hostel.

Wardens manage students, rooms and fees in one place. Students sign in to see open rooms, request one, and track their own fees and complaints.

Built for both sides of the door

For students

Check room availability, send a room request, see what you owe, and raise a complaint.

For wardens

Approve requests, block rooms for repairs, record payments, resolve complaints, and post notices.

For the records

Two students per room, rooms 101 to 120, a full activity log, and backup and restore.

How it works

  1. The warden adds students and sets the hostel fee.
  2. Students sign in and request a free room.
  3. The warden approves the request and the room is assigned.
  4. Fees, complaints and notices are tracked from then on.
Built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Data is saved in this browser's localStorage.
